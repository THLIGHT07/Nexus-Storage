    What NEXUS STORAGE is

    NEXUS STORAGE is a personal dashboard and command center that runs in your web browser. You can keep your favourite web apps and links in one place, write notes, customise themes and wallpapers, check the weather, play small games, and chat with an optional AI assistant.

    This Privacy Policy covers the NEXUS STORAGE website and its backend service (together, the “Service”). “We”, “us” and “our” mean the person or team that operates it — see Contact.

    Using the Service means you accept this policy. If you don't agree with it, please don't create an account.

    Your dashboard data is saved in your browser so the app is fast, and is also stored per user in our PostgreSQL database wherever account sync is enabled for your account. We keep your data separate from other users' data and tie it to your account only.

    Technical data

    Like any web service, our servers see your IP address when you connect. We use it in memory to rate-limit sign-in attempts and one-time-code requests and to stop abuse. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and request path) for security and operations. We don't build profiles from them.

    How we use your information

    • To create your account, sign you in and keep your session secure.
    • To show your own apps, notes, profile and settings back to you.
    • To send verification and password-reset codes to your email when you ask for them.
    • To protect the Service — rate limits, lockouts, blocking abusive accounts and fixing bugs.
    • To meet legal obligations when they apply.

    We do not use your notes, apps or profile for advertising, and we do not train AI models on them.

    Passwords, login and sessions

    Your password is hashed with bcrypt before it is stored, so neither we nor anyone with database access can read it. Please choose a strong, unique password (8–72 characters).

    When you sign in, the server issues a signed JSON Web Token (JWT) that is valid for 7 days. Your browser keeps it in local storage and sends it with requests so you don't have to log in on every page. Changing or resetting your password signs out sessions that were created before the change. Signing out removes the token from your browser.

    All data is sent over HTTPS when the Service is deployed on a secure domain. We also use parameterised database queries, request-size limits and rate limits to reduce common attacks. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

    Email codes and Brevo

    If you link an email address, or use forgot-password / change-password, we send a short one-time code to that address. These emails are delivered through Brevo, a transactional email provider. Brevo receives your email address and the message we send, and handles them under its own privacy policy.

    We only email you for those security purposes — no newsletters or marketing. Linking an email is optional, but without a verified email we can't help you recover a forgotten password.

    The AI assistant

    We don't run an AI model. The assistant works only if you provide your own API URL, model name and API key from an AI provider you choose.

    • Your API key and AI settings stay in your browser. We don't receive, log or store your key on our servers.
    • When you chat, your messages are sent directly from your browser to the provider you configured. That provider's privacy policy and terms apply to those messages.
    • If you turn on web search, search requests go to public sources (such as DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia) through the CORS proxy set in Settings. The default proxy is a third-party service, so only use a proxy you trust, or turn web search off.

    Because the key is saved in your browser, anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to use it. Don't save an API key on a shared computer, and don't paste passwords or other secrets into the chat.

    Weather and location

    If the weather widget is on, your browser asks permission to use your location. If you allow it, your approximate coordinates are sent directly from your browser to the Open-Meteo weather service to fetch the forecast. They are not sent to our servers or saved to your account. You can switch the widget off in Settings → Appearance, or deny the browser prompt.

    Cookies and local storage

    We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Instead, the app uses your browser's local storage (and, for custom wallpapers, browser storage such as IndexedDB) to work properly:

    • Session: your sign-in token and current user.
    • Cache: a copy of your apps, notes, theme, wallpaper, clock and display preferences, so the dashboard loads instantly.
    • AI config: endpoint, model, persona and API key (see above).

    You can clear this any time: sign out, use Reset all data in Settings, or clear site data in your browser. Clearing it only affects this device.

    Third-party apps and links

    Apps you add to your dashboard open external websites in a new tab. Those sites are run by other people and have their own privacy practices. We don't control them and aren't responsible for what they collect. External services the Service itself talks to are limited to Brevo (email), Open-Meteo (weather, optional), your chosen AI provider and search proxy (optional), and standard font and icon CDNs used to display the interface.

    Who we share data with

    We do not sell your personal data, and we do not rent it or trade it. We only share it in these limited cases:

    • Service providers who help us run the Service — our hosting and database provider, and Brevo for email codes — and only as needed for that job.
    • Legal reasons — if the law, a court or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of users and the Service.
    • With your consent — if you ask us to.

    Administrator access

    The Service has a password-protected admin panel. Through it, an administrator can see the list of accounts (such as usernames, status and join date) and can block, unblock or permanently delete an account — for example, to stop abuse or at a user's request. A blocked account cannot sign in until it is unblocked.

    The admin panel is not built to browse your notes or apps. As the operators of the database, we technically could access stored data, and we only do so when it's necessary to maintain, secure or repair the Service, or to comply with the law.

    How long we keep data, and deleting it

    We keep your account data while your account exists. One-time codes are short-lived and replaced or removed once used or expired.

    You can delete your account yourself in Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its profile, settings, apps, notes and pending codes from our database, and clears the app's data on that device. An administrator may also delete an account that breaks the Terms of Use. Backups kept by our hosting provider may hold copies for a limited time before they are overwritten.

    Reset all data (also in Danger Zone) only clears data on this device and keeps your login.

    Your choices and rights

    • Access and correct: your profile, username and settings are all visible and editable inside the app.
    • Delete: remove your account and data at any time (see above).
    • Optional features: email linking, the AI assistant, web search and the weather widget are all optional and can be turned off.
    • Ask us: if you'd like a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion we can't do for you in the app, contact us and we'll help within a reasonable time.

    Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data-protection law. Contact us and we'll do our best to respect them.

    Children's privacy

    NEXUS STORAGE is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as the app grows — for example, when new features change how data is handled. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date above. If a change is significant, we'll make a reasonable effort to let you know in the app. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the new version.
